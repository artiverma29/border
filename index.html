Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

PDF Border is a free tool for adding borders to PDF documents. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it — and the short version is: almost nothing happens to it, because it never leaves your device.

How your files are handled

Every part of this tool — reading your PDF, rendering the preview, drawing the border, and generating the final file — runs locally in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded to a server, stored, or transmitted anywhere. When you close this tab, nothing about your file remains.

Information we collect

We don't operate a backend server for this tool, so we don't collect, store, or have access to your documents, your name, your email address, or any account information. There is no sign-up and no account.

Cookies and local storage

PDF Border doesn't set cookies and doesn't use browser storage to track you or save data between visits.

Third-party resources

To run, this page loads a small number of resources from third-party content delivery networks:

  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — delivers the PDF.js and pdf-lib libraries used to read and generate PDFs.
  • Google Fonts — delivers the Inter typeface used for the interface.

Loading these resources may expose your IP address and basic browser information to Cloudflare and Google, the same way it would for any website that loads a font or script from a public CDN. We don't control what those providers log; you can review Google's privacy policy for details on font requests.

Children's privacy

This tool isn't directed at children and isn't designed to collect information from anyone.
